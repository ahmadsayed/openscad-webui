Frequently Asked Questions

How does the rendering work?

Rendering happens using OpenSCAD WASM (https://github.com/openscad/openscad-wasm). The performance of the rendering is based on your machine's capabilities.

What about privacy?

This website uses DeepSeek v3 as the backbone of the LLM model, so all privacy is subject to DeepSeek's privacy policies - DeepSeek Privacy Policy.

How is my data stored?

Our website may temporarily store your prompts as operations happen asynchronously on ephemeral storage, which resets from time to time. We do not store data long-term.

What if the AI doesn't work?

If the AI does not work, you can still type OpenSCAD code and render online. We will do our best to keep the AI running.

Is there a cost to use this service?

So far this service is free of charge. We may add advertisements in the future to cover the running costs.
